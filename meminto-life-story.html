A memoir becomes possible one story at a time.

A Life Story Book helps a person record the story of their life through thoughtful prompts about childhood, family, work, love, beliefs and defining moments.

Four gentle steps

How it works

The process is guided from the first question to the moment your family opens the finished book.

01

Choose the questions

Start with hundreds of carefully written questions, organized around every season of life. Edit, skip or write your own so the book feels entirely personal.

02

A question arrives

Questions arrive at a comfortable weekly rhythm. Answer in the app, by email or over the phone—by typing, talking, adding photos or recording a video.

03

Answers become chapters

Each answer becomes part of the book. Arrange chapters, add photographs, choose a layout and preview every page before your matte hardcover is printed.

04

The family reads, hears and sees

QR codes in the printed pages play the original voice and video recordings. The ebook can be shared around the world, so everyone can keep the story close.

Made for every storyteller

Speech-to-text

Speak naturally in many languages. Every recording is transcribed into editable text while the original audio stays safe.

Storytelling by phone

No app and no typing required. Call a familiar phone number, hear the prompt and tell the story aloud.

Audio & video QR

A book you can listen to. Scan a discreet code on the page to hear a laugh, a voice or watch the original video.

Simple View

One question at a time in a calm, uncluttered interface—especially helpful for people who prefer fewer controls.

AI tools

Collaboration
